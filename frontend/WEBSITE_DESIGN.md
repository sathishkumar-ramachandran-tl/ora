# Sindhai - Website Design Strategy

## 1. Brand Identity
*   **Name:** Sindhai
*   **Tagline:** Architect Your Ambition. Execute with Intelligence.
*   **Concept:** "The Neural Operating System." It's not just a tool; it's a second cortex.
*   **Visual Language:** 
    *   **Palette:** Deep Slates (`#0f172a`), Electric Indigos (`#4f46e5`), and Neural Emeralds (`#10b981`).
    *   **Texture:** Glassmorphism, subtle grid backgrounds (representing the spatial canvas), and glowing node connections (representing the neural graph).
    *   **Typography:** Inter (Clean, legible, modern).

## 2. Site Architecture (Sitemap)

1.  **Home (Landing Page):** The hook.
2.  **Features (The System):** Deep dive into modules.
3.  **Pricing (Investment):** Personal vs. Enterprise.
4.  **Manifesto (About):** Philosophy of "Constraint Management".
5.  **Login / App:** Direct entry.

## 3. Detailed Page Breakdown

### A. Home Page (Landing)

**Section 1: The Hero**
*   **Headline:** "Stop Managing Tasks. Start Architecting Outcomes."
*   **Sub-headline:** "Sindhai is the first AI-native Operating System that turns your chaotic ambition into a structured execution hierarchy."
*   **Visual:** A 3D or high-fidelity 2D render of the *Neural Graph* component floating in dark space, with nodes pulsating.
*   **CTA:** "Initialize Workspace" (Primary) | "Watch the Neural Link" (Secondary/Video).

**Section 2: The Agitation (The Problem)**
*   **Copy:** "Your tools are fragmented. Your brain is overloaded."
*   **Visual:** A chaotic collage of Jira tickets, Notion docs, and Slack messages with a red "OVERLOAD" overlay, transitioning into the clean Sindhai Dashboard.

**Section 3: The Solution (The Sindhai Way)**
*   **Layout:** Bento Box Grid.
*   **Card 1 (The Incubator):** Screenshot of the Idea Board. Copy: "Capture thoughts instantly."
*   **Card 2 (The Strategist):** Screenshot of the AI Agent generation modal. Copy: "AI that plans *for* you."
*   **Card 3 (The Graph):** Screenshot of the Knowledge Graph. Copy: "See how everything connects."

**Section 4: Social Proof**
*   **Format:** "Trusted by High-Velocity Operators."
*   **Logos:** (Placeholders for MIT, YCombinator, etc. based on personas).

**Section 5: Final CTA**
*   **Copy:** "Your second brain is waiting."
*   **Button:** "Enter Sindhai."

---

### B. Features Page (The System)

**Section 1: The Spatial Canvas**
*   **Focus:** Infinite flexibility.
*   **Visual:** A video loop of a user dragging a sticky note into a column and it snapping into place (The Milanote-style interaction).
*   **Key Selling Point:** "Structure meets Chaos. The only whiteboard that speaks directly to your database."

**Section 2: The AI Agents (Cortex)**
*   **Focus:** Agentic Workflow.
*   **Visual:** Split screen. Left side: User types "Launch Podcast". Right side: Sindhai generates 4 milestones and 12 tasks in real-time.
*   **Key Selling Point:** "Strategist. Tactician. Risk Officer. A full staff in your pocket."

**Section 3: The Neural Graph**
*   **Focus:** Visualization.
*   **Visual:** Interactive D3.js embed (if possible) or static image of the graph.
*   **Key Selling Point:** "Navigate your life like a galaxy, not a spreadsheet."

---

### C. Pricing Page

**Plan 1: Personal (The Founder)**
*   **Price:** Free / $15 mo.
*   **Features:**
    *   1 Workspace.
    *   Unlimited Projects.
    *   Basic AI Agents (Gemini Flash).
    *   Spatial Canvas.

**Plan 2: Enterprise (The Organization)**
*   **Price:** Custom.
*   **Features:**
    *   Unlimited Workspaces.
    *   Team Permissions & RBAC.
    *   Advanced AI Agents (Gemini Pro).
    *   Secure Document Vault (S3/Encryption).
    *   API Access.

## 4. Technical Implementation for Marketing Site

*   **Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router).
*   **Styling:** TailwindCSS (to match the app).
*   **Animations:** Framer Motion (essential for the "Neural" feel).
*   **Hosting:** Vercel.
*   **Analytics:** PostHog (privacy-friendly).

## 5. Design Assets Checklist (Screenshots needed)

1.  **The "God View":** A full-width screenshot of the Executive Dashboard populated with data.
2.  **The "Synapse":** A close-up of the Spatial Canvas with an image card connected to a note card.

## 6. Copywriting Tone

*   **Voice:** Professional, Sharp, Futuristic, yet Human.
*   **Keywords:** Neural, Architecture, Velocity, Constraint, Ambition, Cognitive Load.
*   **Avoid:** "To-do list", "Productivity app" (Too generic). Use "Operating System" instead.
